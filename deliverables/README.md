### Final Project Deliverable

#### Chosen Audience and Rationale

As identified in the first stage of this project (problem framing and scoping), the primary stakeholders of this project would be portfolio managers and algorithmic traders, and students who are studying financial engineering or quantitative finance could be users. This is because the model predictions from this project can be used for market pre-screenings, and the model outputs can be useful in informing buy/sell/hold decisions in a simulated environment. 

#### Why This Format Fits the Chosen Audience's Needs

Slide deck presentations are an effective way to clearly communicate information to a wide variety of audiences, from traders and portfolio managers who have extensive knowledge on this subject, to students who are just learning.
